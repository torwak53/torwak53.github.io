This page is about a game I made with my friend. It's a horror game named BTG.

Intro

The idea was created on the New year 2023/24. We made this dream possible in infinite long 20 months. It was a big suffering but we made our own 3D horror game about my friend's highschool.

The process

Since we were brand new to Unreal Engine where we were working, it took us a lot of time to actually understad some of the principes of the UE (we still don't understand most of them). The plan was to finish it in only one year but it took us eight more months to publish it on itch.io. We were suffering a lot and the scary npcs were such a pain. Regardless that, we made it till the end and we managed to finish the big game BTG.

Summary

I suggest you to learn a lot about an app before you start working on some big project in that app. If you need any tips for working in UE5, feel free to ask but I probably won't be able to help you. If you want to try out our game, visit penthic.itch.io/BTG. The best coworker I could wish for was Penthic.
